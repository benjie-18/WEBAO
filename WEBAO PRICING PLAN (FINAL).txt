TERMS AND DETAILS
[1] Currency and taxes
All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). GST/QST (14.975% in Quebec) and other applicable taxes are extra.
[2] Contract term and cancellation
Initial term: 6 months.
After the initial term: month-to-month.
Cancellation: 30 days' notice. Notice may be given during the initial term; cancellation takes effect no earlier than the end of the initial term.
Annual prepay plans have a 12-month initial term, then continue month-to-month.
Add-ons follow the same terms. Add-ons require an active base plan and end when the base plan ends.
Extra Page is a one-time charge and is not subject to a term.
No setup fee.
[3] Billing options and annual vs monthly pricing
Billed monthly, or annually in advance with 2 months free (annual price = 10 months).
Comparison over 12 months:
Launch: C$89/mo x 12 = C$1,068 billed monthly, or C$890 billed annually (about C$74.17/mo). Annual saves C$178.
Grow: C$149/mo x 12 = C$1,788 billed monthly, or C$1,490 billed annually (about C$124.17/mo). Annual saves C$298.
Scale: C$349/mo x 12 = C$4,188 billed monthly, or C$3,490 billed annually (about C$290.83/mo). Annual saves C$698.
[4] Launch
Designed for solo operators who mainly need a professional online presence and a way for customers to contact them.
Contact form submissions arrive by email.
Positioning: Get online and look professional.
[5] FAQ levels
Basic FAQ section (Launch): a short FAQ section on the page.
Extended FAQ (Grow and Scale): a larger, organized FAQ section with more questions and categories than the Basic FAQ.
[6] SEO levels
SEO Foundations (Launch): unique title and meta description, Open Graph tags, sitemap, Google and Bing indexing submission, LocalBusiness structured data, mobile speed baseline.
SEO On-Page + GBP Setup (Grow): everything in Foundations, plus keyword-targeted copy for your main service and city on each page, internal linking, and a one-time Google Business Profile setup (if not existing) and optimization (categories, services, hours, photos, description).
SEO Expanded On-Page + Service-Area (Scale): everything in Grow, applied to up to 10 pages, with dedicated service-area pages and image / alt-text optimization.
Local SEO Management (add-on, all tiers): ongoing monthly work: GBP posts, citation building and cleanup, review monitoring, local visibility tracking and adjustments.
Dividing line: one-time setup (included in Grow and Scale) versus ongoing monthly work (add-on).
[7] Routine updates and hours
Routine work includes changing hours, information, location and images, and moving or editing existing sections.
Routine work does not include redesigns, new features, technical development and new pages. New pages are billed as Extra Pages (add-on 10).
Included amounts: Launch up to 1 hour per month, Grow up to 3 hours per month, Scale unlimited routine updates, handled in order received. Reasonable-use policy applies.
Time is logged and rounded to the nearest 15 minutes. Unused hours do not roll over.
[8] Support reply times
Support reply times: Launch 3 to 5 business days, Grow 2 business days, Scale same business day (target).
Same-Day Priority Support is the Scale support level.
[9] Grow
Designed for established local businesses that need a website to actively generate leads.
Positioning: Turn your website into a lead-generation tool.
This remains the core webao offer and the C$149 anchor.
[10] Google Business Profile setup
One-time: setup (if not existing) and optimization (categories, services, hours, photos, description).
Ongoing management is the Local SEO Management add-on (add-on 1).
[11] Analytics layers
Basic dashboard (Grow, Scale): live view of what happened: total visits, form and quote submissions (if applicable), call and text clicks, last 30 days.
Monthly Growth Report (included in Scale, add-on for others): delivered monthly, shows what changed: traffic sources, month-over-month changes, bookings, review activity.
Advanced Admin Panel (add-on): live drill-down on why: conversion rates by source and page, views per page, lead funnel, device and location breakdown, performance insights, exports. Aggregate analytics.
[12] Scale
Designed for growing businesses that want their website to become part of their customer-acquisition system.
Positioning: A managed website and lead-generation system for growing businesses.
